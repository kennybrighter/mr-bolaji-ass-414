Who We Are

The Department of Computing is dedicated to teaching computing principles and developing practical skills in software development, digital systems and problem-solving.

Our Vision

To support the development of knowledgeable, creative and responsible computing professionals.

Our Mission

To provide quality computing education, encourage practical learning and prepare students to respond to changing technological needs.

Our Core Values

  • Innovation and creativity
  • Integrity and responsibility
  • Teamwork and respect
  • Continuous learning
